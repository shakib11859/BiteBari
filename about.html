Our Sweet Story

There's a little story behind every bite we bake... 💛

BiteBari started from a small kitchen — not a factory. Just a pair of loving hands, a heart full of passion, and the dream of spreading homemade happiness. 🏡✨

Every cake, every pastry, every jar of sweetness is made with the same care as we'd bake for our own family. No shortcuts, no preservatives — only pure ingredients, love, and honesty in every layer.

It all began with a few cakes made for neighborhood kids. Their joy, their smiles, their "আরেক টুকরো চাই!" — that's where BiteBari found its true meaning. ❤️

We believe that food is not just about taste — it's about memories. And at BiteBari, we don't just bake cakes… we create sweet stories in every bite. 🍰🌿

BiteBari is proudly a Brand under Sikder Agency, dedicated to bringing homemade goodness to your table — fresh, heartfelt, and full of flavor.

Because when it's made from home, it's made from heart. 💫

Our Team

At the heart of BiteBari is a passionate, creative team dedicated to turning every cake into a celebration. Our head pastry chef brings years of experience and a commitment to quality, while our design expert adds artistic flair to every creation. On the other side, our operations and customer manager ensures smooth coordination and timely delivery for each order. Together, we blend craftsmanship, creativity, and care — because for us, baking is not just a job, it’s a way to make people smile.
